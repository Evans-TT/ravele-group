About The Enterprise

Fresh produce grown with structure, pride, and partnership in mind.

Ravele Royal Farming Enterprise (Pty) Ltd is an agriculture-focused company producing and supplying high-demand fruit and vegetables. The farm is positioned for practical growth across production, processing, quality standards, and market operations.

The company welcomes strategic investment, joint ventures, supply partnerships, infrastructure development, and distribution relationships that can expand access to fresh produce locally and beyond South Africa.
